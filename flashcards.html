About States and Capitals

States and Capitals is a pair of free learning games for the 50 United States and their capital cities. The Map Game lets players find states on a U.S. map, name capitals, place states by dragging, and explore facts about each state. The Flashcard Game offers flip cards and quizzes.

Privacy

These games are designed to protect children's privacy.

  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no tracking, and no cookies.
  • The games do not collect or sell personal information.
  • We suggest players use a first name or nickname on the scoreboard.

Player names, scores, and map settings are saved only in the web browser on the device where the games are played. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser's data erases them.

The games are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record basic technical information, such as your internet address, under GitHub's own privacy policy. The games contact no other outside services.
